Frequently asked questions

How do I add my city?

Tap the list icon in the top-left, then use the search bar to find any city worldwide. Tap a result to add it. You can also tap “My Location” to use your current position (you'll be asked for location permission).

What can the AI assistant do?

Tap “Ask WeatherAI” and ask practical questions in plain language — “Do I need an umbrella today?”, “Should I wear a coat?”, “When's the best time for a run?”. It answers using the live forecast for the city you're viewing and can look up other locations and air quality.

Where does the weather data come from?

All weather, air-quality and location-search data comes from Open-Meteo. Forecasts are models and may differ slightly from other apps.

Why does a forecast differ from another weather app?

Different apps use different forecast providers and models, so temperatures and conditions can vary by a degree or two, especially several days out. Neither is “wrong” — they're independent predictions.

How do I switch between °C and °F?

Tap the °C / °F toggle in the top-right of the main screen. The choice is remembered and is also used by the AI assistant.

Is my data private?

We don't require an account. Approximate location is only used to fetch local weather and is never stored on our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
